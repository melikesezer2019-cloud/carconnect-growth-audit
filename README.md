# Growth Audit — CarConnect

Five ranked ideas plus a clickable prototype of CarConnect's onboarding and paywall, rebuilt in CarConnect's own design.

**Live:** https://melikesezer2019-cloud.github.io/carconnect-growth-audit/
**Prototype directly:** https://melikesezer2019-cloud.github.io/carconnect-growth-audit/?open=proto

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Screen | Potential outcome |
|---|------|--------|-------------------|
| 01 | Say what happens next before the price | Compatibility check, car ready | CR +10–15% |
| 02 | Name their car in the paywall headline | Paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 03 | Lead with the plan your website calls Best Value | Paywall | ARPU +10–15% |
| 04 | Put your 4.6 and a real review where they decide | Car ready | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Offer the monthly plan when they close | Offer on close | ARPU +10–15% |

Run together: potential result up to **+25% CR to purchase** and **+30% ARPU**.

## Grounding

Every price, rating, quote and claim comes from CarConnect's own published material: the App Store listing (4.6 from 2.6K ratings; In-App Purchases $9.99, $19.99, $99.99), carconnectapp.com (Weekly $9.99, Monthly $19.99, Annual $99.99 marked Best Value; 46 makes, 618+ models; connected-services steps; the quoted App Store review) and the in-app screens (3-day free trial on weekly, "150K+ drivers").

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for CarConnect.
