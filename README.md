# Growth Audit: CarConnect Digital Car Key

Onboarding and paywall audit of [CarConnect: Digital Car Key](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/carconnect-digital-car-key/id6773911925), based on a 61-second screen recording (Turkish locale) and the App Store listing, 9 November 2026.

**Live page:** https://melikesezer2019-cloud.github.io/carconnect-growth-audit/

Contents: the recorded flow screen by screen, ranked onboarding findings, a paywall teardown with test ideas and expected impact ranges, open questions, and App Store risks.

Patterns are drawn from Adapty's teardowns of top apps across numerous verticals; impact ranges are expected effect, not measured lift for this app.
